# Legacy Bot Brand Polish Plan

## Goal

Prepare an upgraded Legacy Bot avatar that reads clearly at Discord sizes without replacing the current production avatar automatically.

## Avatar Direction

- Keep the LB mark as the primary identity.
- Use a thicker white outline around the letters for better contrast in dark and light Discord themes.
- Preserve a simple circular or badge-style silhouette so the icon remains readable at 32px.
- Avoid fine detail, thin shadows, or small secondary text.
- Export test previews at 512px, 128px, 64px, and 32px.

## Discord Readability Checklist

- LB remains readable at 32px.
- White outline does not blur into the background.
- Shape is recognizable in server member lists and bot profile cards.
- Icon works on dark, light, and blurred Discord surfaces.

## Safe Rollout Notes

- Do not replace the avatar from code.
- Prepare image options outside the bot runtime.
- Manually review in Discord before uploading to the bot application.
- Keep the existing avatar available for rollback.
